  • Texty: <text_blue>Text</text_blue>
  • Špeciálne boxy: <erikai_blue>...</erikai_blue>
  • Hrúbka textu: <thin_red>...</thin_red>, <gross_red>...</gross_red>, <verythin_red>...</verythin_red>, <verygross_red>...</verygross_red>
